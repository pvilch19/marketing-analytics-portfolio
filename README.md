# Marketing Research & Analytics Portfolio

Hi, I'm Pamela Vilchez. I use data to investigate marketing questions, understand consumer perceptions and communicate findings that can inform decisions. This portfolio brings together research interpretation, predictive modeling, retail analysis, pricing and visual storytelling.
